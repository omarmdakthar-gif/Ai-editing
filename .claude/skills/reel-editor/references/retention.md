# Hooks and retention

Patterns taken from what the 10 references actually do. No formula guarantees a viral reel. Test these and let the user's own results overrule anything here.

## The first 3 seconds
Every reference does at least two of these at once:
- **Says the payoff first:** "156 homes sold last year." "You need to be bored."
- **Shows the biggest visual immediately:** a huge number or word on the very first frames.
- **Moves:** the person walks toward camera, or the first cut lands fast.

## Hook formulas that appeared
| Formula | Reference line | Dubai agent version |
|---|---|---|
| Proof number | "156 homes sold last year. That's not luck." | "My client bought below launch price. That's not luck." |
| Us vs them | "Your agent said paying full price was the only option. One of us is lying to you." | "Your broker said this floor was the best one. One of us is wrong." |
| Market question | "Is Dubai real estate market still worth it?" | Use the real question buyers ask this month |
| Secret | "Let me tell you a secret nobody talks about." | "A secret developers don't put in the brochure." |
| Surprise fact | "This one was built in the 1800s, but it sure doesn't look like it." | "This apartment costs less than you'd pay in rent." |
| Wrong belief | "Most people assume... But that's wrong." | "Most people think off-plan is the safer choice. That's wrong." |
| Promise with a list | "Here's how you fix every marriage. You do four things." | "Four checks before you pay any Dubai booking fee." |
| Money math | "You can buy a hundred million dollar building with 30 million in cash." | Use the user's real numbers |
| Why this location | "If you want to know why Nakheel chose this plot, look at what's around this plot." | Use on any launch |
| Bust a sales pitch | "Agents are selling Palm Springs as the cheap Palm Jebel Ali. It's not." | Correct a claim buyers are hearing now, without naming a competitor |

Only use a claim the user can back up. A hook that overpromises loses trust, and for an agent trust is the product.

## Keeping attention
- **Pattern interrupts:** something changes on screen every 1.5–3 seconds: a cut, a big word, a graphic or a new location.
- **Numbered structure:** "First... Second..." or "1, 2, 3, 4" makes viewers wait for the rest.
- **Contrast turn around the middle:** "But if you choose right..." Open a problem, then turn it.
- **Credibility beat around a third of the way through:** "I've been making that call for over 10 years." Keep it to one line.
- **Show the sentence:** when a number or place is spoken, it's on screen.
- **Trim:** speech in the references runs about 190–260 words a minute of talking. Tighten pauses rather than speeding up the voice.
- **Length:** most real estate references run 36–60 seconds. Longer ones need a story or a list to carry them.

## Calling for leads
- Ask for one thing only. Choose DM, comment keyword or call.
- Say what they get: "DM me and I'll guide you on which developer, which project and which floor."
- Make it low risk: "Worst case, you got someone local looking after you."
- Keyword comments such as "Comment OFFPLAN" make replies easy, and each comment is a warm lead to follow up.
- Show the handle, and the phone number if the user wants it, with the permit number where required.

## Sound as a retention tool
- Whoosh into cuts and impact on the big word.
- Riser into a reveal, then a short silence just before the payoff line.
- Pop or tick on list numbers and small graphics.
- Keep effects under the voice. If an effect is noticed more than the words, it's too loud.
