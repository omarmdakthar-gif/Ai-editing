# Editorial style

Studied from ref04, ref05, ref08 and ref10, all from one creator's page. It feels like a magazine or a short documentary. An idea is the hero, and the speaker is one of many visuals.

For real estate, use it for market explainers, the agent's own story, and "why" reels. Swap the paintings and film scenes for Dubai skylines, handovers, old Dubai archive photos, maps and floor plans.

## Fonts
Closest free matches, chosen by eye. The exact originals can't be confirmed from video.
- **Small words:** Inter Tight 500, around 46px, very tight spacing.
- **Big words:** Inter Tight 700, around 112px, spacing so tight that letters touch.
- **Serif emphasis:** Playfair Display 800, around 118px, tighter than a normal serif.
- **Serif italic:** for names and feeling, such as "Arthur Brooks", "happiness" or "French horn".
- Mix them inside a single phrase. A small sans word sitting on a big serif word is the signature.

## Colour
- Electric blue `#1F6BFF` as the one brand colour. Near-white `#F8F7FB` and near-black `#0B070C` as the other two.
- Measured colour is low in saturation overall, so blue screens hit hard when they arrive.
- Each reel can use a single warm or cool grade for its cutaways. Ref10 stays golden and painterly. Ref04 stays blue.
- Pick one brand colour for the user and keep it across reels. It becomes recognisable.

## Captions
- Placed 40–55% down the frame, slightly off-centre, and they move around from thought to thought.
- Word by word on the spoken word, with a soft blur-in.
- One word in each thought goes large and usually changes font.
- Large words get a slow fade rather than a pop. Calm, not jumpy.

## Screens to rotate between
Keep a speaker on screen for about a third of the reel. Fill the rest from this list:
1. Talking head, usually two looks in different lighting. Dark cinematic and white cyclorama in ref05.
2. Solid colour card with one line of text. Blue or white.
3. White page with photos stacking in as they're named, with slight overlap, as in ref05's "I write / speak / teach".
4. Full-screen cutaway that illustrates the sentence literally.
5. Simple diagram: thin-line circles, 2×2 grid, timeline with a date.
6. Document or research paper floating on a colour card, as evidence.
7. Quote card or fake-free phone screen, such as a profile or message mock made from real content.
8. Rounded video card on black, which feels different from full screen.
9. Dark glowing abstract, such as a blue orb or lit brain, for big ideas.

## Motion graphics
- Slatted blinds or vertical strips that wipe between images, as in ref10.
- Particle or paper-grain dissolves between scenes, as in ref04.
- Blur-in, blur-out of whole frames.
- Diagrams draw on with thin white lines.
- Timelines scroll sideways with a date marker.
- Circle highlight around a person in a photo.
- Name highlight, with a blue box behind the first name.
- Numbered lists shown large on screen, then counted through: "1. 2. 3. 4."

## Cutting
- Measured average shot: 1.5–1.8s, very consistent across all four reels.
- First cut within about a second or two. Ref10 cuts at 0.47s.
- A visual change on nearly every thought.
- Literal illustration: the picture shows the noun being said.

## Sound
- Music is quieter here. Pauses in speech sit around 6–10 dB below the voice.
- 66–76% of cuts have a rising high end before them, and around half have a low impact after. These are measured estimates.
- Sound stays gentle and cinematic. Soft whooshes, light paper and tick sounds, no hard hits.
- Master around -17 LUFS.

## Ending
The final line sits on a colour card or dark frame, then fades to a centred Instagram icon and handle. Ref04 uses a comment keyword: 'Comment "PANAS" below and I'll send you the quiz.' This suits lead generation well. For example: comment "OFFPLAN" and get a guide.

## Footage rights
These references use film stills, paintings and other people's photos. The user should use footage they own, licensed stock, public domain images, or AI images made for them. Don't copy film clips into a business's lead generation content.

## Standing elements
Small handle with Instagram icon, top right around 25% down. It moves to the left for some sections.
