# Formats and shot lists

The six reel formats and what to film for each. When the user picks a topic, match it to a format, send them its shot list, and say which style it uses. The full user-facing plan is `PLAN.md` in the project folder.

| Format | Style | What they film |
|---|---|---|
| Location explainer | Map | Selfie only, plus a marked screenshot per place |
| Project comparison | Map | Selfie only, plus a source for every number |
| Market truth | Spotlight, seated or walking | Talking to camera, two framings |
| Numbers breakdown | Spotlight, deal breakdown | Talking to camera, plus a written list of figures |
| Buyer guide | Editorial | Two looks, dark and bright, with spoken numbered lists |
| Property tour | Spotlight, tour | Gimbal glides per room, details, hero shot, permit number |

Every reel ends with one comment-keyword call to action that says exactly what the viewer receives.

## Filming basics for every format
4K at 30fps in standard colour (Rec.709) with HDR video turned off. iPhone HDR (Dolby Vision) can wash out or shift colours in the SDR pipeline and on Instagram. Don't use Apple Log until it has been tested with Apple's LUT on a short outdoor clip and the user prefers the look. Locked exposure and focus. Phone vertical at eye level, 1–1.5m away. Wireless clip mic. Soft light from the front, never a bright window behind. Two seconds of silence before speaking. Ask for the hook three ways and the CTA twice. Ask for short pauses between points, which give clean cuts.

## Per format
- **Map:** chest-up selfie against a plain wall (it shows in a small corner window). One point per place, with pauses. Marked screenshot of every place. Source for every number.
- **Spotlight:** wide walking shot and medium shot of the same lines in different spots. 6–10 five-second b-roll clips: skyline, entrance, keys, contract, floor plan on a laptop, hands on a map. One still hook line against a clean background, for the person cut-out.
- **Editorial:** two looks. Say "Number one, number two" clearly. Use only owned or licensed b-roll.
- **Tour:** walk the rooms in order, 0.5x lens, lights on, curtains open. Get detail moves and a 5-second hero shot. The Trakheesi permit number is required before posting.

**Drones:** flying in Dubai needs a registered drone and permission. Check before using one.

**Busy background on a map reel** (first trial: an office with a city-view window, filmed handheld at waist-up): don't use a small corner window, because it reads as clutter. Put the speaker full screen for the intro, re-hooks and CTA, and use a background-removed cut-out over the map. Cut away to the map between takes to hide framing jumps.

## Known gaps (update as they close)
- Captions for translated speech need phrase-level timing, not word-level, because English words don't line up with Tamil speech.
- The `Reel` composition takes one clip. Spotlight reels need a multi-clip timeline with A-roll, B-roll and punch-ins, so build that before the first one.
- The comment card, news cards and light 3D scenes from the map references aren't built yet.
- No brand sheet yet. Build it after the trial, from the user's choices.
- Every export should come with a post package: cover frame, caption text and hashtags.
