## Facts to confirm before posting
- **Nakheel's project** is being marketed as *Palm Springs*, in Jebel Ali next to Palm Jebel Ali, with access from Sheikh Zayed Road. Nakheel had not issued official launch material as of 1 November 2026 ([Truhauz](https://truhauz.com/nakheel-palm-springs-launch/), [Dubai Haus](https://www.dubaihaus.com/en/blog/palm-springs-nakheel-jebel-ali-dubai)).
- **"Five minutes from Palm Jebel Ali":** the earlier Palm Springs reel routed it at about 10 minutes with OpenStreetMap data. The edit keeps your words in the captions but doesn't put a drive time on the map.
- **Aldar's project:** Aldar Development's CEO said a villa and townhouse community near Dubai Silicon Oasis, with Dubai Holding, will launch by the end of 2026 ([The National, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/property/2026/10/14/aldar-to-launch-major-dubai-villa-and-townhouse-project-by-end-of-year/)).
- **"40 million sq ft":** in February 2026 Aldar and Dubai Holding added a plot of about 4 million m² (about 43 million sq ft) on Dubai's eastern growth corridor ([Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/projects/construction/aldar-dubai-holding-add-two-dubai-plots-to-jv-target-10bln-gdv-hmz13j5k), [Gulf Construction](https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/389762/Aldar,_Dubai_Holding_in_$10bn_expansion_of_JV)). That fits your figure, but no source confirms it's the Silicon Oasis site, and broker sites quote other sizes.
- **Prices, unit mix, 10% down payment:** none of it is official yet for either project. The edit labels it "Expected · subject to official launch".
