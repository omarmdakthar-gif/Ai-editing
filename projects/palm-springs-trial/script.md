## Final lines

**HOOK: say all three, and I'll pick one**
- A. "Nakheel is launching a new villa community on the mainland. Here's why this exact spot matters."
- B. "Why would Nakheel build here, and not downtown? Look at what's around it."
- C. "Everyone's talking about Palm Jebel Ali. The bigger story might be right next to it."

**1.** "This is Palm Springs, Nakheel's new townhouse and villa community in Jebel Ali, about 10 minutes from Palm Jebel Ali, right on Sheikh Zayed Road."

**2.** "It sits right next to Jafza, the Jebel Ali Free Zone, with more than 11,000 businesses."

**3.** "Jafza and Jebel Ali Port together support over a million jobs and about 36% of Dubai's GDP. All those people need homes."

**4.** "About 25 minutes away, Al Maktoum Airport is getting a 128 billion dirham passenger terminal."

**5.** "Palm Jebel Ali is roughly twice the size of Palm Jumeirah, with more than 80 hotels and resorts planned."

**6.** "And Jebel Ali Beach is planned to be Dubai's longest public beach, 6.6 kilometres."

**7.** "Jobs, an airport, an island and a beach, all around one community. And Dubai Marina or Expo City is about 20 minutes away. This is where Dubai is growing next."

**CTA: say it twice**
"Prices aren't official yet. Comment SPRINGS and I'll send you the details the day Nakheel releases them."

**Drive times** are estimates, routed with OpenStreetMap data from Al Maktoum Airport Road on the plot's east edge, without traffic. The site is still empty land, so real times depend on where the entrances end up. That's why every time says "about".

**Left out on purpose:** Palm Springs prices (your deck says from AED 3.2M, Nakheel's pre-launch page says AED 3.8M–9M, and neither is official), the launch date (not confirmed), and the Meadows 1 resale example (a single sale, which is risky under advertising rules).

**Plot outline:** about 8 km², which is large for one community, so the marked area may be generous. It's labelled "approx. site" until Nakheel publishes the official plan.
