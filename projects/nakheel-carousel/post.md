# Who is Nakheel? Carousel: post package

## Caption
The developer that built new land in Dubai is now building villas on the mainland. 🌴

Nakheel: Palm Jumeirah, The World, Jumeirah Islands, Jumeirah Park, Jumeirah Village, and now Palm Jebel Ali and Dubai Islands.

Its newest launch, Palm Springs, sits right beside Palm Jebel Ali, with townhouses from AED 3.8M and villas from AED 7.0M.
